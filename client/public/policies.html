Privacy Policy

At Shree Saraswati Vidhyalay, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our students' and parents' personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you make payment transactions through our online portal.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our portal to pay school fees, we collect details relevant to the student and payment registration, including:

  • Student Name, Student ID, and Class/Grade.
  • Payer name, contact email address, and mobile number.
  • Transaction details such as transaction amount, order ID, and date/time of payment.

2. Payment Information Handling

All online transaction details (card numbers, UPI IDs, net banking details) are processed directly by our secure payment gateway partner, Razorpay, under industry-standard security protocols. Shree Saraswati Vidhyalay does not see, access, or store your credit/debit card numbers or passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to:

  • Record and verify the fee payment against the student's ledger.
  • Generate and email/SMS payment confirmation receipts.
  • Respond to customer support requests and troubleshoot transaction issues.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent parent/student information to third parties. Information is only shared with our payment gateway partner (Razorpay) as required to complete the transaction process and as required by legal authorities.

5. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Cancellation and Refund Policy

1. Policy Overview

Shree Saraswati Vidhyalay's online fee payment portal is designed to facilitate quick and secure fee payments for our students. All payments made through the portal are subject to this Cancellation and Refund Policy.

2. Payment Cancellation

Once a payment transaction is successfully initiated and processed, the transaction cannot be cancelled by the user. Please ensure all student details and payment amounts are verified before proceeding to the final payment checkout.

3. Duplicate or Erroneous Transactions

In case of technical issues or system errors leading to a double payment or an incorrect amount being deducted, the extra amount will be refunded. Parents/payers must notify the school administration within 7 business days of the transaction with proof of payment (such as bank statement and transaction references).

4. Refund Processing Time

Once the double payment or error is verified by the school administration, refunds will be approved and initiated. Approved refunds are credited back to the original bank account or card used to make the payment within 7 to 10 working days, depending on bank processing times.

5. Service and Convenience Fees

Please note that any non-refundable convenience charges, platform fees, or service taxes charged by banks or card networks during the transaction may not be eligible for a refund, except in cases where the system itself failed to record a valid attempt.

Shipping and Delivery Policy

1. Nature of Services

Shree Saraswati Vidhyalay's online fee payment portal provides electronic payment services for school/institution fees, registration, and administrative charges. We do not sell or ship physical goods or merchandise.

2. Shipping & Delivery Methods

Since our services are entirely educational and digital, physical shipping and logistics delivery are not applicable. No physical products will be shipped or delivered to your billing/shipping address.

3. Service Delivery Timeline

Upon successful payment confirmation through the gateway:

  • A digital receipt is generated immediately on-screen.
  • An automated payment confirmation is sent to your registered email address and mobile number.
  • The student's fee ledger/records in the Shree Saraswati Vidhyalay portal are updated dynamically to reflect the paid fees.
